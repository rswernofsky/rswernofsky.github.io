Good Things to Eat With Peanut Butter

Peanut Butter is a delicious spread made from ground peanuts. It is hated by some, but loved by many more. I myself am one of the lucky ones whose taste buds give back positive feedback when in contact with the savory snack. For those who are also PB lovers, and wish to find more ways to incorperate it into their diets, here is a guide for how to pair peanut butter well with other foods.

Classic pairings

  • apples
  • bananas
  • pretzels
  • crackers
  • waffles
  • cookies
  • toast
  • celery
  • granola bars

Somewhat controvertial pairings

  • carrots
  • oatmeal
  • yogurt

Ways to include PB in dishes

  • pb&j sandwich
  • in ice cream
  • peanut sauce
  • in cookies
  • in a smoothie
  • overnight oats
