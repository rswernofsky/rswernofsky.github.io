Sandwiches at Rebecca's Cafe

Rebecca's Cafe is a small breakfast and lunch restaurant on Northeastern University's campus. They offer a large variety of food (for a large variety of college students): panini sandwiches, soups, salads, hamburgers, grilled cheese, mac and cheese, you name it. In particular, I love their made-to-order panini sandwiches, so I made a list of how much I've enjoyed each sandwich I've tried, and which ones I still want to try!

Good sandwiches

  • caprese: creamy, tomato-ey, crunchy. what more could you ask for? this is a classic
  • the nicole: the apples create the perfect mix of sweet and savory
  • rebecca roll: the cranberry sauce is quite nice, and I also get satisfaction from sharing a name with this roll
  • chicken caprese: caprese but with more protein
  • the niall: good balance of caramelized onions with turkey. make sure to grab this one from the pre-prepared section
  • bahn-mi: the tofu is AMAZING. however, the last time I got this sandwich there was something unusually crunchy in it so be warned

Don't order this ew

  • buffalo chicken wrap: maybe it was the particular sandwich I had, but this was way too spicy I almost died

Ones I haven't tried yet

  • JFK
  • BLT
  • grilled chicken pesto
  • tuscan turkey
  • fontega chicken
  • BBQ chicken and bacon
