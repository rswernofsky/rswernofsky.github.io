A list of my favorite lists

For as long as I can remember, writing/recording has been my way of organizing my at times jumbled brain. I have this compelling desire to create lists, practically in excess sometimes (does anyone really need a list of dates they watered their bamboo plant?). Since I already write everything down, why not compile it somewhere that can be potentially helpful to others? Several of my friends have wondered why this hasn’t existed before, but here we have it: the list to end all lists - a list of my favorite lists.
